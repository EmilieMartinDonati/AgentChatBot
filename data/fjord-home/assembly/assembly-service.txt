FJORD HOME - ASSEMBLY SERVICE

The assembly service can be added to any order during checkout or later, up to 72 hours before the delivery slot.

Prices
- Chairs, small tables and shelves: 29 EUR per item
- Beds and sofas: 79 EUR per item
- Wardrobes and kitchen units: 149 EUR per item
- Wall fixing (shelves, wardrobes): +25 EUR per item. The customer must tell us the wall type (concrete, brick, plasterboard).

How it works
Assembly is done by the delivery team right after delivery, during the same slot. For wardrobes taller than 2 metres, a separate appointment may be needed.
The team takes away all packaging for recycling at no extra cost.

Cancellation
The assembly service is refunded in full if cancelled at least 72 hours before delivery. After that it is not refundable.

Damage during assembly
If our team damages the furniture or the customer's home during assembly, the customer must report it on the delivery receipt and send photos within 48 hours. Fjord Home's insurance covers the damage.
