# Fjord Home – Home Delivery and Delivery Slots

Furniture is delivered by our own delivery teams (2 people) directly into the customer's room of choice.

## Booking a delivery slot

After the order is confirmed, the customer receives an email to book a **4-hour delivery slot**:

- Morning: 8:00 – 12:00
- Afternoon: 13:00 – 17:00
- Evening (Paris area only): 17:00 – 21:00, +€19

## Changing the delivery date

- The delivery date can be changed **free of charge up to 72 hours** before the booked slot.
- Between 72 hours and 24 hours before the slot, the change costs **€25**.
- Less than 24 hours before the slot, the date cannot be changed; the delivery must be refused and rebooked, which costs **€59**.
- The new delivery date must be within **60 days** of the original order date. After 60 days, storage fees of €2 per day apply.

## Before delivery

The customer must check that the furniture fits through doors, stairs and lifts. Our team will not hoist furniture through windows.

## Failed delivery

If nobody is home during the slot, the team waits 15 minutes and calls the customer twice. A new delivery costs **€59**.
