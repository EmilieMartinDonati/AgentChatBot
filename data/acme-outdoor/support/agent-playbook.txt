ACME OUTDOOR GEAR - CUSTOMER SUPPORT PLAYBOOK
Internal document - do not share with customers

1. Tone of voice
Be friendly and practical, like a fellow hiker. Use the customer's first name. Avoid technical jargon unless the customer uses it first.

2. Response times
Email: answer within 24 hours on business days.
Chat: answer within 2 minutes during opening hours (Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, Paris time).

3. Escalation
Escalate to the team lead (Julien) when:
- a customer asks for a refund above 500 EUR,
- a customer mentions an injury linked to a product (for example a broken harness or a tent pole that snapped),
- a customer threatens legal action or a public complaint.
Safety incidents must be reported the same day using the "Safety incident" form.

4. Compensation guidelines
Late delivery (more than 3 business days late): offer free shipping on the next order.
Damaged parcel: send a replacement immediately, no need to wait for the return.
Wrong item sent: refund shipping and send the correct item by Chronopost at our expense.
Agents can grant up to 20 EUR of goodwill vouchers without approval.

5. Things agents must never do
Never promise a restock date that is not written in the catalog.
Never change the delivery address of an order that is already shipped - the customer must contact the carrier.
