# Changing the shipping or delivery date of an order

Customers often ask to delay or move forward the shipment of their order (for example because they are travelling).

## When a change is allowed

| Order status | Can the shipping date be changed? |
|---|---|
| `pending` | Yes, freely, to any date within the next 30 days |
| `confirmed` | Yes, freely, to any date within the next 30 days |
| `packed` | Only to a **later** date, maximum 14 days later |
| `shipped` | No – the parcel is already with the carrier |
| `delivered` / `cancelled` | No |

## Rules

- The new shipping date must be a **business day** (Monday to Friday). Our warehouse does not ship on weekends or French public holidays.
- A shipping date cannot be earlier than tomorrow.
- Each order can be rescheduled at most **2 times**.
- Rescheduling is free of charge.

## What the agent should tell the customer

Confirm the new shipping date and remind the customer that the delivery usually happens 1 to 3 business days after shipping, depending on the carrier.
