BLOOM BOX - MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION RULES

What it is
The Bloom Box is a monthly subscription at 24.90 EUR per month. Each box contains 4 to 5 products (at least 2 full-size) chosen according to the skin profile the customer filled in at sign-up.

Billing
The customer is charged on the 1st of each month. The box ships between the 5th and the 8th of the month.

Skipping and postponing a box
A customer can skip one month for free, up to 3 times per year.
A box can be postponed by up to 10 days from the account page, as long as it has not shipped yet.

Cancellation
The subscription can be cancelled at any time, without fees, from the account page. Cancellation must happen before the 1st of the month to avoid the next charge. If the customer was already charged for the current month, the box is still sent and no refund is made.

Changing the skin profile
Changes to the skin profile are taken into account from the next month's box if made before the 25th.

Loyalty
After 6 consecutive months, subscribers receive a free full-size product of their choice. After 12 months, they get a permanent 15% discount on the online shop.
