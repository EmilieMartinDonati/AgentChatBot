# Bloom & Co – Returns and Hygiene Policy

## Why our policy is different

For hygiene reasons, cosmetics that have been **opened or unsealed cannot be returned**, even within the return period. This is required by our health and safety commitments.

## Return window

- **Unopened products** with their protective seal intact can be returned within **14 days** of delivery.
- Gift sets can only be returned complete.
- Subscription boxes cannot be returned; see the Subscription document for cancellation rules.

## Allergic reactions

If a customer has an allergic reaction to a product:

1. Advise them to stop using the product immediately and to see a doctor if symptoms persist.
2. Ask for the **batch number** printed on the bottom of the product.
3. Offer a full refund or store credit, **even if the product was opened**. No return is needed.
4. Log the case in the cosmetovigilance register (see the Product Safety document).

## Damaged or leaking products

Ask for a photo of the damaged product and its packaging. Once received, send a replacement free of charge. Refunds for damaged products are processed within **3 business days**.

## Refund method

Refunds are made to the original payment method. Store credit is offered with a **+10% bonus** if the customer prefers it.
